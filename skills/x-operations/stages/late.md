# Late stage (10k+ followers)

> Outline – expand with experience.

Goal: turn audience into distribution and revenue without losing trust. Same rule as earlier stages: only amplify posts that already earned a conversation ([../strategy.md](../strategy.md)).

- **Distribution**: repurpose best posts into threads, long posts, articles, other platforms; recycle evergreen posts after a few months.
- **Collaborations**: co-hosted Spaces, joint threads, guest posts, cross-promotion with peers of similar size.
- **Launches**: tease → launch post with real screenshots/demo → follow-up with results; keep launch posts spaced 1–2 h from others.
- **Monetization**: products, sponsorships, subscriptions, consulting – disclose clearly.
- **Team / delegation**: reply playbook and tone guide so others can help; owner still approves.
