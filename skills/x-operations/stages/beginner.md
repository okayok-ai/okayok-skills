# Beginner stage (0 – 1k followers)

Goal: look like a real, interesting person in one niche, and get the first few hundred relevant followers.

## Profile setup (day 0)
- **Avatar**: clear face or simple logo. **Header**: what you build / one-line value.
- **Bio**: who you are + what you post about + a hook. e.g. `Building <Product> in public · posts on <Niche A>, <Niche B> · indie dev`.
- **Pinned intro post** (see [../posting.md](../posting.md)): who you are, what you build, why follow.
- Fill location/link; keep the handle and name consistent with other channels.

## Warm-up (new or dormant accounts, week 1)
- First days: browse, like, bookmark, a few replies; follow ~10–20/day.
- Verify email and phone early – unverified accounts get lower limits and more checks.
- Post 1 original post per day from day 2–3.
- Ramp: week 1 ~20 actions/day → week 2 ~35 → week 3+ ~50, vary daily ±20%.

## Reply engine (this is what gets seen)
- Template replies do not travel. "来了来了" / "互关啊" landed at 1–2 impressions on a measured beginner account. See [../strategy.md](../strategy.md) and [../replying.md](../replying.md).
- Put the reply cap on larger in-niche posts from the last few hours, where the author is still answering. One specific point. An A/B test (hot posts vs normal posts) did not confirm the 5–10× lift seen in early anecdotes — median likes were about 6 vs 7 — so treat hot posts as reach, not a multiplier.
- One original post a day that a stranger can answer. Answer those comments in the first hour. A product intro beat the 互关 posts on impressions; the 互关 posts mostly sat under 50.

## Reciprocal follows (互关) — how the count moves at this stage
- This is the current cold start, not a side tactic. Same measured account: ~70 follows of people in the same circle, high follow-back, followers 81 → 179 in two days.
- Reply to their actual post first, then follow. They follow back from the notification; they do not read the profile (profile visits stayed ~86 across 3 months while followers hit ~190).
- Only accounts that still post. Skip empty follow-farms.
- Pace: inside the daily follow cap, spread out. The same account was rate-limited the night it fired follows and replies every ~10 seconds. See [../safety.md](../safety.md).
- After 7 days, unfollow non-reciprocators (≤30–50 per session, never the same day you followed).

## Daily safe limits (beginner)
Caps, clock, and the half-pace after a rate limit: [../schedule.md](../schedule.md). Same sentence every reply is a cooldown, not a tactic. Random 60–180 s. Never a second session at the same time.

## Exit criteria
~1k followers, a few posts with real conversations, a clear niche → move to [mid.md](mid.md).
