# Beginner stage (0 – 1k followers)

Goal: look like a real, interesting person in one niche, and get the first few hundred relevant followers.

## Profile setup (day 0)
- **Avatar**: clear face or simple logo. **Header**: what you build / one-line value.
- **Bio**: who you are + what you post about + a hook. e.g. `Building <Product> in public · posts on <Niche A>, <Niche B> · indie dev`.
- **Pinned intro post** (see [../posting.md](../posting.md)): who you are, what you build, why follow.
- Fill location/link; keep the handle and name consistent with other channels.

## Warm-up (new or dormant accounts, week 1)
- First days: browse, like, bookmark, a few replies; follow ~10–20/day.
- Verify email and phone early – unverified accounts get lower limits and more checks.
- Post 1 original post per day from day 2–3.
- Ramp: week 1 ~20 actions/day → week 2 ~35 → week 3+ ~50, vary daily ±20%.

## Reciprocal follows (互关) strategy
- Search posts with `互关`, `回关`, `follow back`, `#buildinpublic`, `#indiehackers` + your niche; prefer last 24–72 h and small/mid accounts.
- For each: write a reply that responds to their content (see [../replying.md](../replying.md)), then follow. Mention 互关 only occasionally.
- Skip accounts with no posts, pure follow-farms, or off-niche content – they hurt reach.
- After 7 days, unfollow non-reciprocators (≤30–50 per session).

## Daily safe limits (beginner)
- Follows 20–50, replies 20–40, 1–2 own posts.
- Random 60–180 s between actions, 2 batches ≥3 h apart. Details: [../safety.md](../safety.md).

## Exit criteria
~1k followers, a few posts with real conversations, a clear niche → move to [mid.md](mid.md).
