# X (Twitter) account operations

A staged operating manual for an X account. It is the umbrella for everything you do on X: growing followers, replying, posting, and staying safe.

**Golden rules**
- Human in the loop: the owner approves every reply and post that goes out under their name.
- Quality over volume: one thoughtful reply beats ten templates.
- Stay well under X's limits and stop on the first warning (see [safety.md](safety.md)).

## When to use

- Setting up or relaunching an X account.
- Deciding what to do this week to grow (and what *not* to do).
- Writing replies or posts, or designing a daily/scheduled routine.
- Handling a rate limit, folded replies, a verification prompt, or a lock.

## 3. Daily routine (default)

1. Check account health (can you follow? are replies visible?). If anything is off, follow the stop-signal table in [safety.md](safety.md).
2. Batch 1 (midday, local-language audience): 15–25 actions.
3. Own post (evening, see [posting.md](posting.md)).
4. Batch 2 (≥3 h later, e.g. US-morning audience): 15–25 actions.
5. Reply to every reply on your own posts within ~30 min.
6. Log: who you replied to/followed, follower/following counts, any warnings.

Weekly: review what drove follows, unfollow non-reciprocators (see safety.md), update this skill with lessons.
