# Posting

## What to post
- **Build in public**: what shipped, metrics, decisions, failures.
- **Lessons**: one lesson per post, concrete.
- **Tips / how-tos** in your niche; tool lists; short threads.
- **Launches**: real screenshots or a short demo – never mock-ups presented as real.
- **Opinions / questions** that invite replies.

## Cadence
- Beginner: 1–2 posts/day. Mid: 2–3/day + 1–2 threads/week. Always ≤ X's limits.
- Space posts ≥1–2 h apart; launches 1–2 h away from other posts.

## Best times (Asia/Shanghai)
- **12:00–13:30** – Chinese-speaking audience lunch break.
- **20:30–21:00** – own post, before evening engagement.
- **21:00–23:00** – US tech morning (ET), good for English posts.
- Test and adjust with your own analytics. Source: https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-twitter/

## Algorithm notes
Replies and conversation weigh more than likes; external links in the main post reduce reach (put them in a reply from you or the bio). See https://partyfly.github.io/x-algorithm-handbook/zh/
