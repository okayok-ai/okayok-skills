# Posting

## What to post
- **Build in public**: what shipped, metrics, decisions, failures.
- **Lessons**: one lesson per post, concrete.
- **Tips / how-tos** in your niche; tool lists; short threads.
- **Launches**: real screenshots or a short demo – never mock-ups presented as real.
- **Opinions / questions** that invite replies.

## Cadence
- Beginner: 1 original post/day. Mid: 1–2/day. A thread is optional, not daily. Always ≤ X's limits.
- Space posts ≥1–2 h apart; launches 1–2 h away from other posts.
- First hour after posting: answer every comment with a real sentence. A post with no early conversation rarely recovers.

## Best times (Asia/Shanghai)
- **12:00–13:30** – Chinese-speaking audience lunch break.
- **20:30–21:00** – own post, before evening engagement.
- **21:00–23:00** – US tech morning (ET), good for English posts.
- Test and adjust with your own analytics. Source: https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-twitter/

## Original-post craft
- One reader, one idea. Hook in the first line: a number, a decision, a failure, or a question.
- Concrete over clever: real screenshots, real numbers you are willing to share. Never invent results.
- End with something people can answer ("how do you…?"), not "thoughts?".
- To amplify someone else, **quote with your own sentence instead of a plain repost** — a quote is content, a repost is +1.

## Pre-post checklist
- [ ] First line works on its own
- [ ] No URL in the main post (link in first reply or bio)
- [ ] Every number and claim is true and current
- [ ] Linked repo/page actually exists and is public
- [ ] Not an 出摊 template; language matches the slot's audience
- [ ] Owner approved the exact text

## Algorithm notes
Weights and the Premium reach gap: [strategy.md](strategy.md).

- No URL in the main post. Put the link in your own first reply, or in the bio.
- First line is the whole hook (the fold cuts early). One idea. A number or a concrete failure beats a slogan.
- Native video (a real screen recording) can earn watch time. A YouTube link in the main post eats both the link penalty and the video.
- Don't post in all caps. Don't @ the same account over and over.
