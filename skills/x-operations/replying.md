# Replying well

Replies are the growth engine — and the easiest place to look like a bot. The ranker pays most when the author answers you (see [strategy.md](strategy.md)).

## Rules
- **Make it answerable**: a specific detail, a real disagreement, or a question only they can answer. "Great post" and pure agreement get scrolled past. If you can't add something, skip the post.
- **Be early**: prefer posts from the last few hours where the author is still replying. A perfect reply on a dead thread does nothing.
- **If they answer, answer once**, then stop. Don't pile on.
- **Length**: at least 15 Chinese characters / 10 English words; 1–3 sentences is ideal.
- **Language match**: reply in the language of the post.
- **No links in replies**; put links in your bio or pinned post.
- **No template text**: never reuse the same sentence across threads; vary openings and structure.
- **互关 / follow-back sparingly**: at most an occasional light mention at the end, not in every reply.
- **On your own posts**: answer every comment in the first hour with a real sentence, not "thanks".
- Avoid sensitive topics (tragedy, politics, personal conflict) for promotion.

## Praise mode (high-volume batches)
When the goal is follow-backs at volume, keep every reply short, warm, and specific to the author's post:
- Praise or encourage something concrete in *their* post (the product, the result, the decision, the writing). One or two sentences.
- **Never talk about your own numbers or problems** ("my reach dropped", "I'm stuck at 200") and never use growth jargon (曝光、权重、限流、抬起来, "boost the algo") in replies. It reads as self-promotion or complaint.
- Vary openings, length, and structure; no two replies the same in a day.
- **One reply per account per day; never reply to the same thread twice.** Check the log before sending.
- Keep a per-owner blocklist of accounts never to reply to.
- **Never test a script under a real post** (a stray "test" reply is public). Test on your own post or a draft.

## Patterns
- Agree + add: `<specific point> is underrated – I saw the same when <experience>.`
- Question: `How did you handle <specific problem> when <context>?`
- Data point: `For us <metric> moved from X to Y after <change>.`
- Praise: `<specific thing> looks really polished — love how you <detail>. Keep shipping!`
- Light 互关 (rare): `<real reply>… 同在做 <Niche>，互关一下 🤝`

## Before posting checklist
- [ ] Mentions something specific from the post
- [ ] The author could reasonably answer it
- [ ] Not a duplicate of any reply today
- [ ] Correct language, no link
- [ ] Owner approved
