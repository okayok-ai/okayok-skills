# Replying well

Replies are the main growth engine in early stages – and the easiest place to look like a bot.

## Rules
- **Respond to the content**: quote a specific point, add an experience, a detail, or a real question. If you can't, skip the post.
- **Length**: at least 15 Chinese characters / 10 English words; 1–3 sentences is ideal.
- **Language match**: reply in the language of the post.
- **No links in replies** (reduces reach and looks spammy); put links in your bio/pinned post.
- **No template text**: never reuse the same sentence across threads; vary openings and structure.
- **互关 / follow-back sparingly**: at most an occasional light mention at the end, not in every reply.
- **Reply to replies within ~30 min** – conversations boost both posts.
- Avoid sensitive topics (tragedy, politics, personal conflict) for promotion.

## Patterns
- Agree + add: `<specific point> is underrated – I saw the same when <experience>.`
- Question: `How did you handle <specific problem> when <context>?`
- Data point: `For us <metric> moved from X to Y after <change>.`
- Light 互关 (rare): `<real reply>… 同在做 <Niche>，互关一下 🤝`

## Before posting checklist
- [ ] Mentions something specific from the post
- [ ] Not a duplicate of any reply today
- [ ] Correct language, no link
- [ ] Owner approved
