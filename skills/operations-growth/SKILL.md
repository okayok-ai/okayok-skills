# Operations and growth

Ongoing work to get a launched product in front of people and build an audience. Launch is the start, not the finish; growth is a daily loop.

## Channels & account matrix
- Set up a consistent brand presence across the channels your audience uses: a social platform (e.g. X), a dev-focused platform, a community (e.g. a chat server), a content/blog platform, and a video channel. Use one unified handle and display name everywhere.
- A brand (not personal) account where the platform distinguishes them.
- Keep profiles complete: avatar, banner, bio, website link.

## Posting cadence
- **Build in public:** regular posts about progress, releases, lessons, and useful tips. 1–2 original posts/day on the primary channel.
- Keep a pinned intro post (who you are + product lineup + a hook).
- Repurpose: a release becomes a post, a short video, a changelog entry.

## Engagement (see the x-operations skill for the detailed X loop)
- Reply to relevant posts with genuine value first, light product mention second.
- Follow likely-to-reciprocate accounts in your niche at a safe pace.
- Everything posts under the user's name — **draft and get approval before posting or following.**

## Cadence automation
- A daily routine that scouts targets, drafts replies/follows and a self-post, and sends them for approval keeps the loop going without manual scouting. Avoid repeating the same posts/accounts.

## Measure
- Track follower/engagement deltas and which angles/niches convert. Double down on what works; prune what doesn't.
- Tie growth back to product analytics (see web-analytics) to see which channels drive real traffic.

## Safety
- Respect platform anti-spam limits (modest volumes, natural pacing, stop on warnings).
- Never fabricate engagement or invent accounts; never auto-reply/DM without approval.
