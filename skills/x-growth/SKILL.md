# X (Twitter) growth playbook

A repeatable loop for growing an X account and promoting products through **genuine engagement**: reply to relevant posts, follow accounts likely to follow back, and post build-in-public updates.

Everything goes out under the account owner's name, so the golden rule is: **draft first, post only what the owner explicitly approves.**

## 0. Confirm inputs before starting

Do not start scouting until these are clear:

- **Account** – which X account will post (and confirm the browser is logged in to it).
- **Products** – for each product to promote: name, a one-line pitch, and its URL.
- **Language mix** – e.g. "about half English, half <local language>, slightly more local".
- **Target niches** – e.g. AI tools, indie hackers / build in public, the product's own category (photo editing, browsers, note-taking, ...).
- **No-go rules** – accounts or topics to avoid, daily caps, tone constraints.

## 1. Scout targets (read-only)

1. Open X in a browser logged in as the account. If it's the wrong account or logged out, stop and report.
2. Record a **baseline**: followers and following counts.
3. Search each niche on both the **Latest** and **Top** tabs. Prefer:
   - recent posts (last 24–72 h) with real engagement,
   - mid/small accounts (more likely to reply and follow back),
   - posts where one of the products is a natural, relevant fit.
4. Build two lists:
   - **Reply targets** (about 5–8 per day): post URL, author + rough follower scale, one-line summary, which product fits, why it's a good reply.
   - **Follow targets** (about 10–20 per day): mid/small accounts in the niches that are likely to reciprocate (bonus if the bio invites follows).
5. **Do not** post, reply, like, or follow while scouting.

## 2. Draft replies, get approval, post

- Write each reply in the agreed language for that thread.
- **Value first**: answer the question, share an experience, add a useful detail, or ask a genuine follow-up. Then, only if it fits, a light and honest mention of the relevant product with its URL.
- No hard selling, no copy-paste replies, no identical text across threads.
- Send all drafts to the owner as one batch. Wait for explicit approval (edits welcome).
- Post **exactly** the approved text, one at a time with natural spacing.
- Capture each posted reply's URL as a receipt and report them back.

### Reply template

```
<genuine reaction / useful point tied to the original post>
<optional: one sentence on how you approached the same problem>
<optional, light: "I'm building <Product>, <one-line pitch>: <URL>">
```

## 3. Follow at a safe pace

- Follow only accounts from the approved list.
- Space follows naturally (a few seconds to tens of seconds apart); keep daily volume modest.
- **Stop immediately** on any follow-limit, rate-limit, or spam warning and report how many succeeded.
- Follow only – no liking, DMs, or posting in this step.

## 4. Build-in-public posting

- Keep one **pinned intro post**: who you are, the product lineup (one line each), and a hook inviting people to follow along.
- Post **1–2 original posts per day**: release notes, lessons learned, progress, metrics you're comfortable sharing, a useful tip.
- Attach a **real screenshot** of the product when it helps. Never use mock-ups presented as real.
- Space a launch/announcement post an hour or two away from other posts.
- Draft → owner approval → post exactly → capture URL.

### Intro post template

```
<one-line who you are / what you do>
I'm building:
• <Product A> – <one-line pitch>
• <Product B> – <one-line pitch>
Building in public – follow along 👇 <URL>
```

## 5. Daily cadence

- Run a scheduled daily routine that **prepares** (never auto-posts): new reply drafts, a follow list, and one self-post draft, then sends them to the owner for approval.
- Execute only after approval.
- Keep a simple log of posts replied to and accounts followed so nothing repeats across days.
- Track follower/following deltas against the baseline to see what's working.
- If the browser/computer is offline or X is logged out, skip the day quietly.

## Safety rules

- **Approval gate** on every reply, post, and follow batch – it's the owner's name on all of it.
- Respect X's anti-spam limits: modest volumes, natural pacing, stop on any warning.
- **Never fabricate** engagement numbers, testimonials, or results, and never invent accounts or URLs.
- Don't reply to sensitive topics (tragedies, politics, personal conflicts) to promote anything.
- Don't post personal information about anyone.

## Iterate

- Review weekly which replies and niches brought follows/engagement; double down on those.
- Unfollow or drop targets that never reciprocate (at a safe pace).
- Refine each product's pitch line as positioning sharpens.
- Update this playbook with what you learn – PRs welcome.
